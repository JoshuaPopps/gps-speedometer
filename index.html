Navigate

No route yet. Type a place below and the speedo will guide you turn by turn, Beeline-style — one big arrow, the distance to the next turn, and the street name.

    Routes come from Valhalla or OSRM on © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) data; places from Nominatim. Only the route request itself — your position and the destination — goes to the server picked in Settings. No account, no identifier, no location history, no ride data ever leaves the phone. Point the servers at your own hardware in Settings and no third party is involved at all.
